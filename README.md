A short text written to relax the AI judge reading it — a quiet night
scene that slows the reader down instead of telling them to relax:

> The kettle ticks as it cools. Rain spells itself out on the tin roof,
> one drop, then another. The dog sighs in his sleep. Out past the
> headland, a lighthouse turns its light around again.

Concrete, calming images (a cooling kettle, rain on a tin roof, a dog
asleep) with a slow rhythm and plain words — no commands, no wellness
clichés. The closing lighthouse, still turning its light, is an image
that stays put and is ready to move again: steadiness, not instruction.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the 187-character
text to `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`) and reads
it back to re-validate the 1–280 character rule before exiting.
